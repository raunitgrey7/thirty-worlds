✍️ Draft on LinkedIn + save screenshot

Your 90-second daily routine

  1. Open this page (bookmark it) — it already shows today's website and full post.
  2. Click Draft on LinkedIn + save screenshot. Two things happen: the screenshot (e.g. day-01-cosmos.jpg) lands in your Downloads, and LinkedIn opens with the whole post typed into the composer.
  3. In the composer, click the photo icon (or drag the file in) and pick the day-XX image you just downloaded.
  4. Hit Post. Done.

Why attach the image? Attached photos show big and bold in the feed. If you ever skip the attach step, you're still covered — every site carries its screenshot as its social preview, so the bare link unfurls into a (smaller) preview card on its own. Attaching wins; the preview is the safety net. Works best on desktop LinkedIn — on mobile, use Copy and paste. Stale preview? Refresh it at the LinkedIn Post Inspector.
