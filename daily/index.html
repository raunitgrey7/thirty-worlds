✍️ Draft on LinkedIn

Your 60-second daily routine

  1. Open this page (bookmark it) — it already shows today's website and post.
  2. Click Draft on LinkedIn — LinkedIn opens with the whole post typed into the composer.
  3. Wait a second for the link preview card (the screenshot) to attach, then hit Post. Done.

The screenshot attaches itself automatically — every site now carries its screenshot as its social preview image, so pasting the link is enough. Works best on desktop LinkedIn; if the composer ever opens empty, use Copy text and paste. If a preview looks stale, refresh it once at LinkedIn Post Inspector.
